✳ THE BOOK

One human.
More range.

Human 2.0 is a field guide to working with agents while staying responsible for what they do.

The book starts with the mechanics, moves into the real work of a six-agent lab, and ends with a practical operating loop: direct, route, execute, verify, and learn.

Its central question is simple: when execution expands, how do we make human judgment stronger too?
