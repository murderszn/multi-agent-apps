01 / THE BOOK

MORE CAPACITY.
MORE RESPONSIBILITY.

Human 2.0 is a field guide to working with agents while staying responsible for what they do.

It begins with the mechanics, moves through a working six-agent lab, and ends with a practical loop for directing work, checking evidence, and learning what to keep.

START SIMPLE

You don't need the full lab to begin. Pick up an account on Hermes, OpenClaw, Meta Muse, or Instinct and you get an assistant with its own computer.

Add machines later. The discipline in this book — direct, route, execute, verify, learn — works at any size.
